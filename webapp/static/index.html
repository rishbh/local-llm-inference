Before you start

This is an experimental test build, not a medical service.

  • This is not medical advice. A small AI model writes the answers. In our own testing it got roughly 4 in 10 answers right and was confidently wrong on the rest, including inventing drug and gene names that do not exist.
  • Do not use it for a real decision about your health or anyone else's. Ask a doctor.
  • In an emergency, stop and call 112 (or your local emergency number). Do not type it here and wait.
  • Do not enter personal details — no names, phone numbers, addresses, or anything identifying you or a patient.
  • Be careful with photos. If you attach an image, do not include a face, a name band, or anything in the frame that identifies a person. The model reads images far less reliably than text.
